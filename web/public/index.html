RQ3: Key Reuse

Unresolved research question regarding client keypair reuse.

Unresolved Finding
The client implementation fell back to generating a fresh keypair per attempt. True ML-KEM keypair reuse was not empirically tested. The null result reflects fresh vs fresh comparison.

Limitations & Reproducibility

Scientific Safeguards & Limits

  • Single-source IP; bounded to 1000 attempts / 30 seconds.
  • Localhost/Docker environment with no real Internet path.
  • Handshake-only payload, no record-layer processing measured.
  • D1 Cookie absent in OpenSSL 3.5.x for ML-KEM-768.
  • Results specific to OpenSSL 3.5.x default provider.
